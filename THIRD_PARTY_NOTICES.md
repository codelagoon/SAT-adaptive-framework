## ArguMentor debate-core

Source: https://github.com/TeslaNeuro/ArguMentor  
Copyright (c) 2026 Arshia Keshvari  
License: MIT

Mastery Engine adapts the debate phase model, skill dimensions, phase sequencing,
and skill averaging from `packages/debate-core/src/types.ts`. The local version
removes Zod, adds Public Forum sequencing, and operates without an AI provider.

## Argdown core

Source: https://github.com/argdown/argdown  
Author: Christian Voigt and contributors  
Package: `@argdown/core`  
License: MIT

Mastery Engine adapts Argdown's relation vocabulary and graph semantics:
support, attack, entailment, contrariness, contradiction, and undercut. The
local graph builder maps a debate card's warrant, counterargument, and response
onto those relations.
