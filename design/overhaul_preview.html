Conditional probability

Prerequisites: sample spaces · basic probability

The concept

“Given” changes the group you're considering. The denominator becomes the number of outcomes in that smaller group—not the entire population.

Worked example

A group has 20 students. 12 like math. Given that a student belongs to this group, the chance that they like math is: 12 ÷ 20 = 0.60 or 60%

Check your understanding
