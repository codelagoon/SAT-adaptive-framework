Conditional probability

When the question says “given,” restrict the denominator to the specified group. The condition changes the sample space.

A club has 12 members who like math and 8 who do not. Given that a student is a club member, the probability they like math is 12 / (12 + 8) = 0.60.

Check your understanding: What does the word “given” change in the denominator?
